Open RV for Windows
Publisher: CompStart.pro

This installer packages an Open RV build configured with -DRV_ENABLE_ALL_CODECS=ON.

The FFmpeg in that build is configured with --enable-gpl and is linked to x264 and
x265 (GPL-2.0-or-later) plus libvpx and libaom (BSD). The combined binary is a
GPL-licensed work. Redistribution must satisfy the GPL, including offering
corresponding source. This build does not pass FFmpeg --enable-nonfree.

Several enabled codecs (H.264, H.265, AAC, AC-3, MPEG-2, DNxHD, and others) are
patent-encumbered. Shipping the binary may require a patent license in your
country. This notice is not legal advice.

Proprietary camera and I/O SDKs (Apple ProRes decoder SDK, RED R3D, ARRI RAW,
Blackmagic RAW, Blackmagic DeckLink, NDI) are not included.

See docs/build_system/release_codecs_and_signing.md in the source tree.
